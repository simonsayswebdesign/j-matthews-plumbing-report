4How to track your traffic & enquiries

You can check these yourself whenever you like, on your phone or a computer. I’ll also send you a simple report each month during the trial.

⚑Your Google listing (calls, directions, website clicks)

This is the quickest one to check, and it’s where most of your calls come from.

  1. Make sure you’re signed in to the Google account you use for your Business Profile.
  2. Search Google for J Matthews Plumbing & Gas Louth, or open the Google Maps app and tap your profile picture, then Your Business Profiles.
  3. Tap Performance.
  4. You’ll see calls from your listing, website clicks, direction requests, how many people viewed your profile, and the searches people used to find you.

Change the date range at the top to compare months.

📈Your website (visitors & enquiries)

You now have view-only access using Jmatthewsplumbing999@gmail.com. Google has emailed you an invite: sign in with that account.

  1. Go to analytics.google.com, or download the free Google Analytics app.
  2. Home shows how many people visited your site.
  3. For enquiries, go to Reports → Engagement → Key events. The event called qualify_lead is your enquiries: each one is someone tapping Call or WhatsApp, or sending the enquiry form, on your website.
  4. Reports → Engagement → Pages shows which pages people look at most.
  5. Reports → Realtime shows who’s on your site right now.

New data can take a day to appear in the reports.

🔍What people search to find you

Google Search Console shows the searches your website appears for, such as “boiler repair Louth”. You have access to this too, with the same Google account.

  1. Go to search.google.com/search-console and choose jmatthewsplumbing.co.uk.
  2. Open Performance.
  3. Queries lists the searches. Clicks is how many people came to your site from each one.

A brand-new site usually takes a few weeks to start appearing. These numbers grow over the trial.

☎Help me measure it properly

A tap on Call isn’t always a booked job. Some people tap and change their mind, and some ring you after reading your site without tapping anything. If you jot down roughly how many new customers say they found you on Google or your website, we’ll get a true picture of what the trial is bringing in.
